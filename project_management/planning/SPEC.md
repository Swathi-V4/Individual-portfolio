# SPEC.md — AI Product Engineer Portfolio

## Project Title
Swathi Veerapalli — Aspirational AI Product Engineer Portfolio

---

# Core Goal

Create a modern, visually engaging portfolio website that communicates:

- who I want to become
- what kind of work I want to do
- what problems I want to help solve
- what project concepts represent my direction
- what tools and skills I am developing
- how I use AI as part of a thoughtful workflow

The portfolio should feel:
- intentional
- future-facing
- human-centered
- visually immersive
- concise and attention-grabbing

The site should NOT feel:
- corporate
- generic
- text-heavy
- overly academic
- resume-like

---

# Professional Direction

The portfolio represents an aspirational career direction in:

## AI Product Engineering

Focus areas:
- AI-powered products
- interactive digital experiences
- UX/UI systems
- product thinking
- human-centered AI
- educational/productivity tools
- creative technology

The portfolio should communicate:
> “I am intentionally building toward this direction.”

NOT:
> “I already work professionally in this field.”

---

# Audience

The intended audience includes:
- professors
- classmates
- creative technology teams
- AI-focused startups
- UX/product design audiences
- future collaborators/employers

The site should feel believable and professionally grounded for a student portfolio.

---

# Tone & Voice

The writing style should be:
- concise
- confident
- modern
- thoughtful
- visually driven
- easy to skim quickly

Avoid:
- long paragraphs
- repetitive explanations
- buzzword overload
- robotic AI language

The portfolio should feel:
- personal
- intentional
- human
- clear

# Layout Direction

## Important Rule
The portfolio should prioritize:
- attention-grabbing structure
- strong visual hierarchy
- fast readability
- scroll rhythm
- modular content blocks

Every section should answer:
> “Why should the viewer keep scrolling?”

- responsive layouts

Motion should feel:
- elegant
- atmospheric

NOT:
- chaotic
- distracting
- overwhelming

# Required Sections

## 1. Hero Section

Purpose:
Immediate emotional hook.

Must include:
- large typography
- short impactful statement
- concise supporting text
- CTA buttons
- animated background atmosphere

Main message:
> AI products should feel human, calm, and useful.

---

## 2. Vision Section

Purpose:
Quick emotional statement.

Large centered typography with minimal text.

Example direction:
> AI should feel less overwhelming and more human.

---

## 3. Who I Want To Become

Focus on:
- future direction
- AI Product Engineering
- human-centered technology
- product thinking
- interaction design

Avoid:
- resume language
- excessive biography

---

## 4. What Kind Of Work I Want To Do

Use fast-scanning cards.

Focus on:
- AI learning tools
- productivity systems
- interactive experiences
- human-centered interfaces

---

## 5. Problems I Want To Help Solve

Use a visually dramatic section.

Focus on:
- academic overwhelm
- confusing AI tools
- stressful digital experiences
- disconnected technology

Tone:
emotionally intelligent + human-centered.

---

## 6. Project Concepts

Projects should represent:
- future direction
- product ideas
- AI systems
- interaction concepts

Projects may be:
- conceptual
- prototype-based
- aspirational

Avoid pretending projects are fully deployed products if they are not.

---

## 7. Skills & Tools

Focus on:
- Product & UX Design
- Front-End Development
- AI Workflow Skills

Should feel:
- believable
- developing
- realistic for a student

---

## 8. AI Workflow Section

Purpose:
Show intentional use of AI tools.

Important:
Do NOT imply blind dependence on AI.

Communicate:
- iteration
- refinement
- research
- prototyping
- editing
- decision-making

Main idea:
> AI supports the workflow, but final decisions are made intentionally.

---

## 9. Credibility / Closing Section

Purpose:
End with confidence.

Tone:
- aspirational
- grounded
- reflective
- intentional

Main message:
> I am not pretending to be finished. I am building with direction.
